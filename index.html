About

Performance marketing, with a growth strategist's eye and an analyst's discipline.

I'm a performance marketing professional with 10+ years of experience driving revenue growth through full-funnel paid media, campaign strategy and performance analytics across Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn. My work centers on optimizing multi-platform budget allocation and holding every dollar accountable to ROI — mostly for e-commerce and consumer brands, alongside running my own profitable, self-funded business.

Along the way I've scaled daily orders from 300 to 1,000+ (a 233%+ increase), lifted order confirmation rates from 75% to 85%, and managed $633K+ in ad spend that produced 340K+ conversions with strong cross-platform ROAS.

What sets the work apart is the second half of it: I convert campaign data into board-ready growth decisions using Power BI, SQL, Excel and Python. I don't hand off a spend report and walk away — I build the dashboard, find the pattern, and tell you what to change next.
